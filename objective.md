# Project Objective

## Objective

The primary objective of this project is to improve the phishing awareness of Internee.pk employees through an authorized and controlled security awareness simulation.

The project will use simulated phishing scenarios to evaluate how employees respond to suspicious emails and identify areas where additional security training may be required.

## Key Goals

### 1. Increase Phishing Awareness

Educate employees about common phishing indicators, including:

* Suspicious sender addresses
* Unexpected attachments
* Urgent or threatening language
* Suspicious links
* Requests for sensitive information
* Unusual login or password-reset requests
* Emails that create unnecessary urgency

### 2. Conduct an Authorized Simulation

A controlled phishing simulation will be conducted only after obtaining the required authorization and defining the scope of the exercise.

The simulation should be designed as an awareness exercise rather than an attempt to obtain real credentials or compromise employee accounts.

### 3. Measure Employee Awareness

The simulation will collect non-sensitive interaction metrics to understand employee behavior.

Examples include:

* Number of recipients
* Delivery rate
* Open rate
* Link interaction rate
* Phishing-report rate
* Training-page visits

These metrics will be used to identify general awareness trends rather than to publicly identify or punish individual employees.

### 4. Identify Security Awareness Gaps

The results will help identify areas where employees may need additional training.

For example, if many participants interact with suspicious links, future training can focus on:

* Verifying URLs before clicking
* Checking sender information
* Recognizing social-engineering techniques
* Reporting suspicious emails

### 5. Provide Security Training

After the simulation, employees should receive appropriate awareness guidance explaining how to recognize and report phishing attempts.

The training should focus on practical actions employees can take when they receive suspicious messages.

### 6. Generate a Security Awareness Report

The final report will summarize:

* Campaign scope
* Methodology
* Overall results
* Awareness observations
* Identified risks
* Recommended improvements
* Future training recommendations

## Scope

The project covers:

* Authorized internal phishing-awareness testing
* Controlled email simulations
* Employee awareness measurement
* Security-awareness education
* Aggregated campaign reporting

The project does **not** include:

* Real credential harvesting
* Unauthorized account access
* Malware deployment
* Collection of employee passwords
* Testing outside the approved scope

## Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

1. The simulation is completed within the approved scope.
2. Employee interaction metrics are successfully collected.
3. Security-awareness gaps are identified.
4. Employees receive appropriate phishing-awareness guidance.
5. A final security-awareness report is produced.
6. Recommendations are provided for improving future security training.

## Authorization

All testing activities must have documented authorization from the appropriate Internee.pk stakeholders before the simulation begins.

Employee data must be handled securely and should not be committed to a public repository. Dummy data should be used in repository examples.
