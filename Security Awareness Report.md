## 6. Key Findings

The campaign results should be analyzed to identify general awareness trends.

Potential findings may include:

### Phishing Recognition

Employees may demonstrate different levels of ability to identify suspicious emails.

### Link Awareness

Link interaction rates can indicate whether employees are checking URLs and sender information before interacting with an email.

### Reporting Behavior

The number of users who report the simulated email can help measure the organization's phishing-reporting culture.

### Training Opportunities

If a significant number of users interact with simulated phishing content, additional awareness training may be beneficial.

## 8. Recommendations

Based on the campaign findings, the following security improvements are recommended:

### Employee Training

Conduct regular phishing-awareness sessions covering:

* Suspicious sender addresses
* Malicious or unexpected links
* Urgent requests
* Unexpected attachments
* Fake login notifications
* Social engineering techniques
* Safe reporting procedures

### Phishing Reporting

Provide employees with an easy mechanism to report suspicious emails to the security or IT team.

### Regular Simulations

Conduct periodic authorized phishing simulations to measure improvement over time.

### Technical Controls

Where appropriate, strengthen technical email-security controls such as:

* Spam filtering
* Malware scanning
* URL protection
* Attachment scanning
* Email authentication mechanisms
* Security monitoring

### Continuous Monitoring

Monitor security-awareness metrics over time to determine whether training is improving employee behavior.

## 9. Awareness Training Message

Employees should remember the following principles:

**STOP → CHECK → REPORT**

**STOP:** Do not immediately click links or open unexpected attachments.

**CHECK:** Verify the sender, URL, context, and request.

**REPORT:** If an email appears suspicious, report it using the organization's approved reporting process.

The simulation should be reviewed after completion to determine:

* Whether the reporting process is easy to use.
* Whether technical email controls require improvement.
* How future simulations can be improved.

## 12. Conclusion

The phishing-awareness simulation provides an opportunity to evaluate and improve employee security awareness in a controlled environment.

The results should be used to strengthen employee training, improve phishing-reporting procedures, and enhance technical email-security controls.

The long-term goal is to create a security-conscious workforce that can recognize suspicious communications, avoid unsafe interactions, and report potential phishing attacks quickly.
